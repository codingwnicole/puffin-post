Depth and motion

Depth comes from paper. Sheets stack, tabs join the page, a desk lamp warms the top-left corner, and cards cast real shadows. Motion copies handling paper: dealing, settling, flipping, sliding a drawer. Every movement reacts to something you did. Reduce motion turns all of it off.

Radius: chips 6, controls 10, tabs 12 (top corners only), sheets 14, dialogs and trading cards 18. Trading-card corners follow each card theme.

  • e1 sheet: the main sheet, scene cards, modules.

Measurements

  • Sheet padding 40 at desktop, 16 on phones. The sheet sits 12 from the window's top and bottom and joins the sidebar with no gap. A second sheet sits behind it, offset 8/6 and turned 0.35°.
  • Dashboard: character columns at least 330 wide, 18 gap. Scene cards have 12/14 padding and a 3-line excerpt in Newsreader 15.
  • Scene drawer: 560 wide, full height, slides in from the right. Full screen on phones.
  • Character icon: 40 in lists, 64 on the Details view, radius about a quarter of the size. Trading card: 5:7. 250 in the grid, 330 on the profile and public page, 150 in the editor's small preview.
  • Touch targets: 40 minimum on desktop, 44–50 on phones.

Fonts, icons and assets

  

    
Fonts (Google Fonts, OFL)
  • Young Serif 400 for headings. It has one weight, so hierarchy comes from size.
  • Figtree 400–700 for all UI text.
  • Newsreader 400 + italic for thread posts, drafts and the public profile's prose.
  • Trading cards keep their own per-theme fonts from TradingCard.tsx, so card downloads look the same as today.

    
Icons
  • The live site already uses Chikin Variety Glyph, Jumpicon and Communication Glyphs icons under CC BY 4.0, credited in the sidebar footer. Keep those credits wherever the icons appear.
  • The glyphs in this file are stand-ins on a 24px grid. Swap each for its Chikin equivalent at build time.

    
Character images
  • Icon: square, 600×600 or larger. Used on the Details view, scene cards, filters and menus.
  • Card portrait: the larger image on the trading card. JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 4 MB. GIFs stay animated; keep them near 400×400.
  • The two stay separate so a busy GIF never ends up in a 40px list icon.

  

  
Public profile pages

  

    
What exists
  • /c/<slug>, server-rendered by public-share.ts. Card or stats style, toggles for moodboard, playlist and thread stats, and an option to let search engines index it.
  • New link (old link stops working), a preview image upload, embed snippets in HTML, BBCode and Markdown, and Stop sharing.

    
What the redesign changes
  • The hero carries the trading card at 330px, with tilt and flip, over a soft halo in the character's favorite color.
  • Sections (about, fun facts, stats, moodboard, playlist, tracklist) sit on a 12-column grid with the same tokens as the app. They're inlined in the server HTML so the page still works without JavaScript.
  • Tilt is the only script: about 1 KB, and skipped under reduce motion and on touch screens.

The check found two problems, both fixed: the main sheet grew taller than the window instead of scrolling, and character-name links were under 44px tall on phones. The live build needs the same min-height: 0 on the sheet and its wrapper.

    
What makes it hold up when it's live
    
      
  • Only one thing scrolls at a time: the main sheet on desktop, the page on phones. The sidebar and desk rail scroll on their own only when they're taller than the window.
    • 
      
  • Layouts respond to the width of the app container, not the device, so the sidebar collapsing at 1020 and the tab bar at 760 work the same in a narrow window as on a tablet.
    • 
      
  • Every text column has min-width: 0, so a long Tumblr handle or tag wraps instead of pushing the page sideways. Only tables and code scroll sideways, inside their own box.
    • 
      
  • Dialogs and the scene drawer cap their height at the window and scroll inside. The tab bar and bottom sheets pad for the phone's safe area.
    • 
      
  • Type, cards and gaps use fixed steps with a few clamp() headings, so browser zoom to 200% reflows instead of clipping.
    •
